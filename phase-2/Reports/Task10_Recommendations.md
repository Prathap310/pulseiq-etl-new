# Task 10 ? Budget vs Actual Recommendations

## Ranking method

Departments are ranked by the sum of positive monthly variances only.
Variance = actual payroll cost - budget payroll cost.
A positive variance means overspend; a negative variance means underspend.
Months under budget do not offset months over budget. Records suppressed
by the employee privacy threshold are excluded from variance ranking.

## Recommendation 1 ? Department D01

- Months over budget: 8
- Total positive monthly variance: ?4,926,386.46
- Largest monthly overspend: ?1,383,966.92

**Recommendation:** Review the payroll breakdown for the largest overspend
months, separating headcount changes, overtime pay, variable pay, and employer
contributions. Compare each driver against the approved staffing plan and
payroll assumptions before proposing a corrective budget or staffing action.
The current variance identifies where to investigate; it does not establish
the cause by itself.

## Recommendation 2 ? Department D04

- Months over budget: 9
- Total positive monthly variance: ?2,609,956.20
- Largest monthly overspend: ?421,346.52

**Recommendation:** Introduce a monthly budget-versus-actual review and a
next-month payroll forecast. Investigate repeated overspend months by checking
staffing levels, overtime, variable pay, and payroll adjustments. Escalate
material unexplained differences to the budget owner for review.

## Limitation

These recommendations are investigation actions, not confirmed root causes.
KPI traffic-light yellow tolerances remain pending confirmation from the
project owner. Missing task-completion targets must remain unscored.
